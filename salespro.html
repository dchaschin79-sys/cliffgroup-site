Create an order in under a minute

  1. Open Customers and pick the customer.
  2. Create a new order and search by SKU, name, or barcode.
  3. Add quantities, confirm totals, and save the order.
  4. Finish on the completed order screen with the order number visible.

How invoice, portal, and payment work together

  1. Open an order and create an invoice.
  2. Send the invoice by email and open the customer portal link.
  3. Show the branded portal and the Pay Online button.
  4. Finish on the paid invoice state back inside SalesPro.

How inventory, purchasing, and shipping stay connected

  1. Show low stock or a backorder-driven replenishment draft.
  2. Create a purchase order and receive inventory.
  3. Create a shipment and send a customer update.
  4. Finish on the final fulfillment status for the order.
